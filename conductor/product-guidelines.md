# SMART-DEORBIT System - Product Guidelines

## Prose Style: Academic & Formal
All technical reports, mission summaries, and in-app tooltips must maintain an academic and formal tone. Language should be precise, objective, and suitable for peer review or institutional evaluation. Avoid colloquialisms or overly simplified metaphors; prioritize the use of standard orbital mechanics and neural network terminology (e.g., "eccentricity," "autograd," "zonal harmonics").

## Branding & Aesthetic: Modern & Sophisticated
The system's interface should project a modern and sophisticated image. While maintaining a professional corporate standard, the design should incorporate subtle high-tech elements that reflect its advanced AI foundations. Use a clean, refined color palette with clear typography to ensure that complex data remains the primary focus.

## UX Principles
- **Physics First**: Prioritize accuracy and transparency in all calculation steps. Users should have clear visibility into the physical assumptions (e.g., atmospheric model, gravity order) underpinning every prediction.
- **Compliance-Driven Layouts**: The primary call-to-action (CTA) across all optimization views must center on the 5-year re-entry mandate. Success metrics should be immediately visible and tied to regulatory compliance.
- **Visualization-Rich Dashboard**: Heavy use of interactive Plotly graphs for trajectory exploration and Pareto front analysis. Visual data representation is the primary method for user engagement and decision-making.

## Tone & Voice: Consultative & Helpful
The system's feedback, alerts, and help sections should be consultative and helpful. Rather than just reporting errors, the system should guide the user through complex optimization trade-offs, explaining *why* certain parameters (like ΔV) might be insufficient to meet lifetime targets.
